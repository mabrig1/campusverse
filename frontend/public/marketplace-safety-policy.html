Marketplace Safety & Ownership Policy

Version 1.0 · 21 October 2026 · Applies to all sellers, buyers and Graduation Sale listings.

Key rule: You may list only goods you own or have explicit lawful authority to sell. A seller's declaration does not mean CampusVerse has verified the goods, seller, payment, or ownership.

1. Seller responsibilities

By publishing a listing, you declare that you own the goods or are authorised by the owner to sell them; that the description, condition, images, price and source are truthful; that the goods are not known or suspected to be stolen; and that they may lawfully be sold. False declarations, fabricated receipts, impersonation, counterfeit goods and misleading serial-number claims are prohibited.

2. Prohibited items and conduct

Stolen, unlawfully obtained, counterfeit, prohibited or unsafe goods must not be offered. Do not sell another student's belongings without permission, remove identifying labels to evade checks, reuse other sellers' photographs deceptively, solicit off-platform advance payments through fake escrow links, or threaten reporters. CampusVerse may reject or restrict listings that violate this policy.

3. Phones, laptops and higher-risk items

Buyers should ask about receipts or other reasonable ownership evidence, inspect the product in person at a safe public location, check device account/activation-lock status and functionality, and compare identifying details where lawful. Keep original invoices or legitimate evidence available for a private review if requested. Do not publish full IMEI numbers, serial numbers, student ID images, BVN or NIN in public listings. CampusVerse currently does not provide independently verified IMEI/police-database clearance, student identity validation or guaranteed inspection.

4. Safe handover and payments

Meet at a well-lit public campus location when possible, bring a trusted companion, inspect goods before accepting them and retain transaction records. Never disclose OTPs, passwords, bank PINs or recovery codes. No seller should describe a payment as CampusVerse-guaranteed unless the checkout explicitly identifies a currently functioning verified payment service. Payment and escrow demonstrations are not equivalent to a regulated, operational consumer-protection service.

5. Report suspected theft or fraud

Use the marketplace listing's Report option to flag suspected stolen property, impersonation, fake receipts or other scams. Give factual details, dates and any available transaction reference; avoid publishing accusations or victims' personal information. For imminent danger or a theft in progress, contact university security or relevant emergency authorities directly. A report is not proof of wrongdoing.

6. Review, restrictions and appeals

Reports may lead to temporary visibility restrictions, requests for additional evidence, cancellation of listings or account action following proportionate human review. CampusVerse should inform the affected seller of the concern when safe and lawful, provide a way to respond, and permit appeal. False or malicious reports are prohibited. Authorities may be contacted only when justified and legally appropriate. This version is a policy commitment; automated restriction and appeals workflows are not yet implemented.

7. Privacy and retention

Collect only information needed for a specific safety decision, restrict access to authorised reviewers, protect data in transit and at rest, and retain evidence only as necessary under a published retention schedule and applicable law. Do not request sensitive government identity numbers through unverified forms. This page does not itself establish that these controls or a secure evidence-submission portal have been deployed.

8. Scope and changes

This policy covers all campus marketplaces, including graduation clear-outs and second-hand bundles. CampusVerse may revise it with a version number and effective date and should communicate material changes. Applicable consumer, criminal and privacy laws continue to apply. This policy does not guarantee an item is authentic or legally owned.
